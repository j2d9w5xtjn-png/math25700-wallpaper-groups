# Wallpaper Groups in Escher's Style

Interactive symmetries of the 17 wallpaper groups (Math 25700). **Based on the periodic drawings of M. C. Escher**, his "regular divisions of the plane": each tiling is made by Escher's method, from one tile whose edges are bent in matching pairs, and each one links to a drawing of Escher's with the same group (14 of the 17 groups). For each group the page shows the lattice, the rotation centers, mirrors and glide axes, and it animates the symmetries, so a tracing of the tiling moves and lands back on it.

The tiles, faces and colors are new drawings made for this page, not copies of Escher's work. M. C. Escher's works are © The M.C. Escher Company B.V., Baarn, the Netherlands. None of them is reproduced here; the links open them in the company's own gallery, https://mcescher.com/gallery/symmetry/.
